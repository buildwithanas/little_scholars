Our Story

About Little Scholar Islamic School

Founded in 2005, Little Scholar Islamic School was established with a passion for providing quality education and nurturing children in a safe, supportive, and morally grounded environment. The school was officially registered and approved in 2010 and currently offers Nursery and Primary education.

In 2015, our Primary section moved to its permanent site in Akuro Phase 2, providing a dedicated environment for learning and growth.

Over the years, our pupils have participated in various academic competitions and, by the grace of Allah, have achieved remarkable success. We continue to encourage every pupil to learn, compete, grow, and strive for excellence.

At Little Scholar Islamic School, we combine academic excellence, good character, discipline, and Islamic values to prepare our pupils for a bright future.

Our Mission

To create an Islamic learning environment that promotes the development of knowledge, confidence, skills, and understanding, helping our pupils grow into happy, healthy, confident, and responsible individuals. We provide a positive learning environment where every pupil is valued and supported to achieve their potential.

Our Vision

To develop pupils who demonstrate respect, integrity, resilience, and independence, preparing them to become confident and responsible future leaders.

A Word From Our Principal

Welcome to Little Scholar Islamic School

On behalf of our entire school community, welcome. Whether you are a parent considering Little Scholars for your child or simply learning more about who we are, thank you for taking the time to get to know us.

We believe every child deserves an education that nurtures the mind, strengthens character, and stays rooted in strong Islamic values.

Our teachers work closely with every pupil to build confidence, curiosity, and a genuine love for learning, while daily prayers, Qur'an, and good conduct remain central to school life. We see education as a partnership — and we're grateful for the trust you place in us to help shape your child's future.

We warmly welcome you to visit our school, meet our teachers, and see firsthand the environment we have built for your child to learn, grow, and thrive.
