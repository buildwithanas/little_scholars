About Us

Little Scholar Islamic School was established in 2005 and officially registered and approved by the relevant authorities in 2010. The school currently operates two academic sessions, catering to both Nursery and Primary levels.



In 2015, the Primary section was relocated to its permanent site, marking a significant milestone in the school's growth. Over the years, the school has actively participated in various extracurricular activities, and by the grace of Allah (Alhamdulilah), has consistently achieved notable success and recognition.
