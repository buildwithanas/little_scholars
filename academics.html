Our Approach to Learning

Building Strong Foundations for Lifelong Learning

At Little Scholar Islamic School, we provide a balanced education that goes beyond textbooks. Our curriculum is designed to develop academic knowledge alongside critical thinking, communication, and creativity, so every pupil learns to understand the world, not just memorise it.

Alongside strong academics, we place equal weight on problem-solving, confidence, discipline, and collaboration — the everyday skills that help a child thrive both in the classroom and beyond it.

Learning Beyond the Classroom

Child-Centred Learning

Lessons are designed to encourage participation, curiosity, and active learning.

Practical Learning

Pupils are encouraged to learn through activities, demonstrations, projects, and real-world examples.

Critical Thinking

Students are encouraged to ask questions, analyse information, and develop problem-solving skills.

Collaborative Learning

Group activities help pupils develop communication, teamwork, and leadership skills.

Monitoring Every Child's Progress

We track each pupil's development through a consistent, transparent process so parents always know how their child is doing.

Continuous Assessment
